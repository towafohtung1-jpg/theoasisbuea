Welcome

A Private Retreat Like No Other

Tucked away in Mile 16, Buea — at the foot of Mount Cameroon — The Oasis is a private guest house compound unlike anything else in the Southwest Region.

A full-size swimming pool with jacuzzi. A poolside bar and lounge. Fully furnished apartments and studios. 24-hour security. Solar power. All within a gated compound that feels like your own private retreat.

Whether you're climbing the mountain, exploring Limbe's beaches, visiting the university, or simply escaping the everyday — The Oasis is where you come to rest, refresh, and reconnect.

Discover

Explore Southwest Cameroon

The Oasis sits at the crossroads of the Southwest Region's best experiences. Use us as your base camp and explore everything this incredible region has to offer.

Mount Cameroon

West Africa's highest peak, right at your doorstep. Whether you're training for the Race of Hope or doing a guided day trek, The Oasis is the perfect place to rest before and after your climb.

15-20 min to trailheads

Limbe Beaches

Golden sand, warm Atlantic waters, and the famous Down Beach seafood experience. A perfect day trip — swim in the ocean by day, swim in our pool by evening.

30-45 min by car

Limbe Wildlife Centre

One of Central Africa's most important primate conservation centres. Home to gorillas, chimpanzees, and other rescued wildlife. A must-visit for families.

35-45 min by car

Buea Colonial Heritage

Explore Buea's colonial-era architecture, the historic Bismarck Fountain, and the lush mountain landscapes that make this town one of Cameroon's most beautiful.

5-15 min

University of Buea

Cameroon's premier Anglophone university. Visiting for graduation, conferences, or campus tours? The Oasis is the most comfortable accommodation nearby.

10-15 min

Botanical Garden

The Limbe Botanic Garden — one of the oldest in Central Africa. Tropical plants, nature trails, and stunning coastal views. Perfect for a peaceful morning visit.

35-45 min by car
